How the pieces meet

Turn on Show glue surfaces to spread the pieces apart. Amber marks both faces of each joint on the cut ribs and outer rim. Follow the adhesive directions for which face to coat.

Adhesive for PA12 nylon

Ask the print shop about Permabond TA4550, a two-part acrylic that the manufacturer recommends for nylon 12. Dry-fit the four pieces first. The adhesive cures fully in about 24 hours at 73°F (23°C).

Before your cat uses the cover, have the shop check the cured joints after wetting and test the cover on a ½-inch ledge for a 9 lb cat landing near the center.
